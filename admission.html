Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to enquire about admission.

1

Visit the School

Visit the school office to enquire about available seats and admission requirements.

2

Collect Information

Obtain the admission form and confirm the required documents from the school office.

3

Submit Documents

Submit the completed form and the documents requested by the school.

4

Assessment & Confirmation

Follow the school's instructions regarding any assessment and admission confirmation.
