Recognising the education leaders who guide policy, strengthen institutions and support learning across Sindh — from provincial direction to district coordination and school leadership.

A SHARED COMMITMENT

Working Together for Every Learner

Strong education systems are built through shared responsibility. Provincial leadership sets direction, departmental and regional teams support implementation, district officers coordinate school operations, and school leaders bring educational priorities into everyday learning.

Provincial Direction Policy, access and educational priorities
Departmental Leadership Planning, systems and implementation
District Support Coordination, supervision and school support
School Leadership Teaching, learning and student development
